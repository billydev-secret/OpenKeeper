# Privacy & Data Retention

## What's kept

Activity, not content: XP, coins, game history, and a record that a message happened — who, where, when. **Not your message text, and not the pictures you post.** Two exceptions, both handed over on purpose: a Guess Who photo (until solved, or **7 days**) and an image you buy as an emoji or role icon.

It sits on hardware in this server's own home, and none is sold. Outside services see a request, never who sent it: **Anthropic** for the AI assistant, **Spotify** and **YouTube** for music, plus a small helper YouTube's player needs. The web dashboard is the exception: if you use it, it reaches you through **Cloudflare**, which carries your signed-in session.

## When you leave

A few minutes after you go, by itself: **what you wrote and the pictures you posted are deleted**, and your activity is **kept but anonymised** — your account becomes a number, your names *Cleared user 7*. It can't be undone.

Moderation, DM-consent and no-contact records keep your real account — they protect someone else. Jailed, or with a ticket open, nothing runs at all: that's moderation business. And if the bot is offline when you go, it never learns you left — ask for an erasure instead.

## "Anonymous" means anonymous to members

Confessions and anonymous submissions carry your name in a log only the admin can see, for **90 days**. Whispers are not in that log — they keep your name until you clear them under **My Data** on the Help & Settings panel.

## Your tools

**My Data** on the Help & Settings panel clears your own messages — all, just pictures, or just text — your whisper history, and your Guess Who data. **Delete everything** there wipes your messages and Guess Who data and anonymises the rest, so you start over as a new member; anything in progress (a bet, a game, a voice room, a Pen Pals pairing) is lost. `/guess optout`, `/whisper optout`, `/bank mute` and Wellness's opt-out each leave one feature.

## Your data, or an erasure

**The Golden Meadow** is responsible for your data. Ask in <#TICKET_CHANNEL_ID>; no reason needed. An erasure keeps a few things on purpose, and tells you exactly which — the coin ledger, moderation and DM-consent records, a no-contact order protecting someone else, and a bug you filed, minus your name. Unhappy with how it was handled? You can complain to the data protection authority where you live.
