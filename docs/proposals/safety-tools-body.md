# Safety Tools

Ways to protect yourself. None is rude, and you never have to say why.

## From another member

**Block them** — Discord's own block, from their profile. Stops their DMs and hides their messages everywhere, not just here. They aren't told.

**No Contact** (on the Help & Settings panel) — closes every route the bot could use to put you together: DMs, pairings, games, matching, voice rooms. Silent, and it reaches where blocking doesn't: a feature that would have paired you quietly does something else, and they never see a refusal naming you.

**A voice-room block** — if you host a room, you can keep someone out. These do different jobs; you don't have to pick one.

## Your DMs

**DM permissions** live in <#DM_PERMS_CHANNEL_ID>: **Closed**, **Ask** or **Open**, changed as often as you like. Rule 5 is that DMs are opt-in, so Closed is normal, not a statement.

## Stepping back from one feature

**My Data** on the Help & Settings panel → **Whisper history** clears every whisper you sent or received here; `/whisper optout` stops new ones. `/guess optout` leaves Guess rounds, `/bank mute` stops the economy's DMs, and Wellness has its own opt-out.

## When you need a person

**Open a ticket in <#TICKET_CHANNEL_ID>** — someone bothering you, a copy of your data, an erasure, a correction. Private, logged, straight to the mod team. Screenshots help; reasons aren't needed.

What's kept, and for how long: **Privacy & Data Retention**.
